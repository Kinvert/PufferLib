Pixel RL: complete checkpoint curves

Each game, training appearance and evaluation drawing is a separate comparison. This viewer shows audited observations; it does not run models or certify superiority.

Hover or focus a point to inspect its checkpoint and both score bounds.

Lines follow successive declared checkpoints, including declines; missing checkpoints break the line. They are visual guides, not interpolation estimates. Pong has separate lower and dashed upper curves plus vertical censoring ranges: these are deterministic possible-score bounds, not confidence intervals or midpoint estimates.

Black rings mark stored descriptive lower/upper time-frontier flags only for complete conditions and paired means. They always refer to the entire assigned model set, even if a model is hidden. No frontier is recomputed from visible models, individual seeds or decision-axis views. Partial conditions have no frontier rings. No confidence bands or dominance certificates are enabled.

Collection provenance and qualification


Means require every assigned seed at the same checkpoint and average their recorded training times equally. There is no best-seed envelope, selected test checkpoint or cross-game score average. Runtime/math acceptance, learner/cap calibration, held-out exclusion and statistical inference remain separate gates.
